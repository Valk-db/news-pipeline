Recompute it yourself

Anyone can verify this proof with only a SHA-256 tool. Start from the canonical payload bytes above: their SHA-256 is the leaf hash. Then walk the levels: at each level the node's own position decides the order. An even position hashes as the left child, an odd position as the right child, always as sha256(0x01 || left || right). The final digest must equal the checkpoint's Merkle root below.
